<objective>
Wire the two classic scripts into `index.html`'s `<head>` and record in `CLAUDE.md` what was just
built, so the next agent does not have to reverse-engineer it.

Load order is the whole point of this plan. A classic `<script>` in `<head>` executes at parse time;
module evaluation is dependency-graph-driven and strictly later. That gap is exactly where this
app's documented cold-start failure mode lives — a stale ES module whose export list changed hangs
the app at "Loading application..." with an empty console. Only a handler already attached at parse
time sees it.

Purpose: `window.Sentry` becomes live, early, on the one page that matters.
Output: 4 new lines in `index.html`; 3 new bullets in `CLAUDE.md`.
</objective>
