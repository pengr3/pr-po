<objective>
Deploy the tightened rules and prove, in the live application, that server-side enforcement now binds the scoped roles without breaking anything.

This is the moment the phase's central risk either materialises or does not. `113-RESEARCH.md` establishes that Firestore `list` denial is total: if any surface was missed, a scoped role's access to `projects` collapses entirely rather than degrading. Every preceding wave exists to make that outcome impossible — the client conversions shipped first, the completeness audit disposed of every audited site, and plan 113-09's browser checkpoint verified the narrowed queries against production data while the permissive rule was still in force.

The rollback is one command: redeploy the previous `firestore.rules` from git. Rules apply instantly and no index change ships in this deploy, so recovery is seconds, not minutes.

Purpose: land the enforcement and confirm the defect that produced this phase is actually dead.
Output: production serving the tightened rules, with a recorded UAT result.
</objective>
