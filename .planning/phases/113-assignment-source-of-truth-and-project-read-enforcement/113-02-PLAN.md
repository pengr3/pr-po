<objective>
Deploy the additive indexes and rules from plan 113-01 to production, in that order.

This gate exists because Firestore `list` denial is total, not partial (`113-RESEARCH.md`, Rules Mechanics: "Security rules are not filters — queries are all or nothing"). Shipping those conversions before this deploy would break the exact role this phase exists to unbreak.

Composite indexes are deployed by a different command than rules and take minutes to build on a live database. They go first.

Purpose: make the server side ready before the client side moves.
Output: production `clmc-procurement` serving the widened rules and the 3 new indexes.
</objective>

| T-113-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | n/a | No packages installed in this plan. |
