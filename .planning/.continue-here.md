## Decisions made (answered this session)

1. **Content capture = Form-fill at generation** — a generate modal collects the narrative/table
   fields, saves them onto the project/service doc (regenerable), then renders. Mirrors PO `promptPODocument`.
2. **Signatures = All internal only** — no client-acceptance blocks; COC is a CLMC-issued certificate.
3. **Scope = Both projects AND services (parity)** — build into both files together → extract shared module.
4. **Gate rule = Generate OR attach link** — gate check becomes `<doc>_url present OR <doc> generated`;
   keep the external-URL box as a fallback.
